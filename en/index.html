AUSMAT Study Notes

A full-course assessment timetable, subject weightings and content covered. Bilingual, printable.

AUSMAT is the Australian Matriculation programme: a pre-university year taught in Malaysia that sits the Western Australian WACE / ATAR. This site documents one intake of it, covering five AT subjects: Mathematics Methods (MAM), Human Biology (HBY), Physics (PHY), English EAL/D (ELD) and Computer Science (CSC). Unfamiliar abbreviations are defined in the glossary.

This site contains only general information I compiled and explanations I wrote. It does not include school-issued handouts, worksheets, exam papers or their solutions. It holds no personal information, with one exception: the advising tool does ask for a student record, but it runs entirely in your own browser, uploads nothing and stores nothing on a server. The mind map does name some course materials and their page counts — that is an index of what I have, not the materials themselves.
