Past Papers

Where to find past WACE papers for the five subjects

Two sources cover all five subjects: the official SCSA pages (free, with marking keys and formula sheets) and WACE Vault (community-run, with a longer run of years).

SubjectOfficial papers (SCSA)Community vault
MAM · Mathematics MethodsSCSAWACE Vault
HBY · Human BiologySCSAWACE Vault
PHY · PhysicsSCSAWACE Vault
ELD · English (EALD)SCSAWACE Vault
CSC · Computer ScienceSCSAWACE Vault

Between them the two columns cover 2009–2025. The official pages give the paper, the marking key and the formula sheet for each year, starting from 2020; the vault goes further back (2010 for most subjects, 2009 for Computer Science), and for Mathematics Methods it adds a questions-sorted-by-topic folder that beats paging through years when drilling one concept. **The exact start and end years differ by subject — see the table above.**

The “community” column is a third-party collection, not an official source. It was found to carry commercial textbook PDFs and internal school test papers alongside past exams. This site neither hosts nor endorses that material; the link is an entry point only. If you want to stay clear of it, use the SCSA pages above — free, no login, 2020 onwards.

Note: the SCSA domain is scsa.wa.edu.au. Plenty of material online writes scsa.wa.gov.au, which does not exist.
